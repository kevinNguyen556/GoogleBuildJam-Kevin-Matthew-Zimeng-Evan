## About

Googleheim is a local-first web app that transforms your photo library into an interactive memory map and a walkable 3D museum. It is wrapped in a cozy, postal-themed scrapbook aesthetic and uses the Gemini API to automatically organize, tag, and search your photos.

Your library is saved in your browser's **local storage**, so your personal photos stay on your device and under your control.

## Features

### Smart photo library
- **Automatic organization:** Gemini sorts your photos without manual folders.
- **AI tagging:** photos are labeled by people, places, objects, and mood.
- **Natural-language search:** find photos by describing them, like "the beach day with the red umbrella."
- **Local-first storage:** your photo library is saved in your browser's local storage, not on a server.

### Memory map
- Explore your photos pinned across an interactive world map.
- Browse your memories by place and time.

### Walkable 3D museum
- Step inside your photos as a 3D gallery.
- Wander through your memories as exhibits.

### Play with your memories
- **Group timelines:** collaborate with friends on shared timelines.
- **Photo scavenger hunts:** play location-based hunts using your photos.
- **Digital time capsules:** bury memories at spots on the world map to find later.
- **Animated couriers:** send photos to friends via playful postal couriers.
